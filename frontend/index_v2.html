Ranking Methodology

Startup Radar ranks recent, publicly verifiable momentum signals. It is a discovery tool, not investment advice or a prediction of company success.

Eligibility

A company is only ranked once its headquarters region, primary domain, and industry are verified, it has at least one qualifying event within the last 180 days, its data confidence is at least 60/100, and its evidence isn't based solely on a single weak discovery source. Companies that are shut down, acquired, or a large public incumbent are excluded, as are ones whose identity is still ambiguous (e.g. confused with a similarly named company).

Three independent regional lists

United States, India, and Rest of World are ranked completely separately, each against its own candidate pool — not a single global list split up afterward. If fewer than 50 companies in a region qualify, the list simply shows fewer than 50; nothing is invented to fill it out.

Scoring components (v2)

  • Recent verified events — 25%
  • Product & customer traction — 20%
  • Stage-adjusted funding momentum — 15%
  • Verified hiring momentum — 15%
  • Market expansion & partnerships — 10%
  • Technology differentiation / moat — 10%
  • Independent source corroboration — 5%

Data confidence, data completeness, and AI relevance are shown separately and are not part of the momentum score.

Recency decay & stage adjustment

Older events contribute exponentially less (a ~45-day half-life), so momentum reflects recent movement, not lifetime history. Funding amounts are scored relative to comparable peers at the same stage and region, not compared raw — a strong seed round can outrank an ordinary late-stage one.

Source tiers & confidence

Sources are tiered (company/official/regulatory, established journalism, or discovery-only) and syndicated copies of the same story are counted once, not multiple times. Data confidence combines source reliability, corroboration, freshness, and completeness — an unpopulated or "Unknown" field is never counted as complete.

Known limitations

This is a news- and public-record-driven discovery feed, not a licensed data provider — it will miss startups that haven't received qualifying press coverage. Employee counts are shown as ranges, not exact figures, when only that level of confidence is available. Version: score_version v2.
